# MyFigaro

## Product overview

MyFigaro is a working AI operator for founders and small businesses. It brings research, communication, scheduling and software work into one conversation, keeps business context and returns concrete results for the owner to review.

The owner stays in control of consequential actions. The product can run on a dedicated machine or a hosted instance.

## Current stage and development history

The product is functional and is being prepared for its market launch. Its development has taken approximately six months and has involved several contributors.

This existing development history is disclosed. We do not claim that the entire product was created during the Colosseum hackathon. The founder is entering the competition as a solo participant.
